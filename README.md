One sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — using only words and pictures available in 1800.

> Picture a market square where a thousand voices cry out at once from every stall, and you may answer any of them — and hear their reply before the bell tolls twice.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` gates the sentence (one sentence, 1–200 chars, not whitespace-only), writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt`), reads it back and verifies it byte-for-byte, exiting non-zero on gate failure.
